## Publishing rhythm

- **Monday:** flagship article or case study.
- **Wednesday:** SQL, Python or BI walkthrough supporting the article.
- **Friday:** concise LinkedIn summary with one finding, one limitation and a link.
- **Monthly:** review engagement, project quality and target job descriptions; adjust upcoming topics.

## Editorial quality gate

Before publishing, check that each article has a clear objective, original explanation, reproducible sample, validated calculations, limitations, meaningful takeaways and working internal links. Do not publish synthetic results as real-world results or employer achievements.
