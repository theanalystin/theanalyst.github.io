What connects the markets

  • Rates: Federal Reserve expectations and US Treasury yields can change global equity valuations and capital flows.
  • Currency: A stronger US dollar can add pressure to emerging-market currencies and imported input costs.
  • Energy: Crude oil moves matter for India's trade balance, inflation outlook and energy-sensitive sectors.
  • Earnings: Guidance from large technology, financial and consumer companies can shift sector sentiment across regions.
  • Policy: RBI, Fed and government decisions should be assessed against the market's prior expectations.
