## What this site is about

I’m building my practice around a simple idea: good analysis should help someone make a better decision. That means understanding the business question, defining metrics carefully, validating the data, explaining what changed and communicating what to do next.

My professional background spans business reporting and governance, analytical work, operations and category-focused roles. I hold an MBA in Operations & Marketing from the Institute of Public Enterprise and a B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering from VFSTR.

## Topics I write about

- Business performance and KPI governance
- SQL and reproducible analysis
- BI dashboards and executive reporting
- Commercial and operational analytics
- Python-assisted reporting and data quality
- Consulting frameworks and analytical communication

## Approach

Articles use sample, public or synthetic data. I document assumptions and limitations and distinguish illustrative results from verified business outcomes. Employer-confidential information is not published.

The site is a work in progress: the aim is to share useful learning, build stronger analytical practice and make each project easy to inspect and reproduce.
